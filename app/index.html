Help

The project autosaves in this browser.

New project clears the video and all recordings — save a copy first if you want to keep them.

Save project writes a .audiorec file (video + recordings). The first time it asks where to save; after that — or after Open project — it overwrites the same file. Browsers that can't write files directly download a new copy instead.

Export saves Audio only (a WAV) or Video + audio (an MP4 with the original picture; the range must end within the video). Both contain the mix of the tracks that are neither muted nor hidden, without muted clips. Choose the start and end; by default they are the two export markers on the timeline ruler, which start at 0 and at the timeline Length (the video length unless you set one). Silence is added where nothing plays. The video's own audio is mixed in only if you tick it.

Enable REC on the track to record on (one track at a time), press Record and speak while the timeline plays, over the video if you chose one. Pausing or seeking stops the recording.

Space play / pause · * start / stop recording · F full screen

Mute on the Video audio row silences the video's own sound. It only affects what you hear; Export has its own option for the video audio.

Drag a clip to move it; drag its edges to trim start and end. Drop it on another track to move it there at the same time position. Hold Alt while you start dragging to duplicate the selected clips instead.

Double-click a track name to rename it. Double-click a clip to open its settings: name, gain (fader or typed dB), fade in and fade out in ms. Under each track volume is its pan; click either value to open the fader with the pan slider, and type a value to set it exactly (dB, or L/R 0–100, C for centre).

The + button above the track names adds a track. The ⋮ button on a track opens its menu, with its color and Remove track.

The fx button opens a compact effect list. Drag the handles to reorder effects, or use Alt+Up/Down on a focused row. Click an equalizer to open its editor: drag knobs vertically or edit their values; Shift gives finer adjustment. The graph shows the EQ response and a live 30-band spectrum during playback. The compressor has threshold, ratio, knee, attack/release, input/output gain and dry/wet mix knobs, with a transfer curve and live input/reduction/output meters. It uses the browser's native dynamics processing, without external sidechain or adaptive timing. Add delay (feedback/tone), reverb (Hall, Room or Plate), pitch shifter, stereo chorus or a downward expander from the same list. Their editors use the same knobs and editable values. Pitch shifting preserves duration but not formants; the expander requires AudioWorklet on localhost or HTTPS. Extend the timeline/export range if you want complete delay and reverb tails. Click outside a dialog to close it. Effects and their order are saved with the project and applied to mixed exports, not individual clip downloads. Each row in the list shows its position in the chain and has an on/off button and a remove button (Ctrl+Z undoes it); the fx button shows how many effects the track has. In the equalizer graph, drag the numbered points to change a band's frequency and gain (L and H are the cuts). The switch next to the title turns the effect on or off; the ring around each knob shows its value. The low and high cuts have no resonance: set their frequency and choose a slope of 12, 24, 36 or 48 dB/oct. The pitch shifter joins the audio where the waveforms match; its Window sets the length of the joined pieces and, like the expander, it needs AudioWorklet (localhost or HTTPS).

Click a clip to select it, Shift+click to select more, or drag a box over empty space (Shift adds to the selection); Del or the trash button removes the selected clips, Esc clears the selection.

C or the scissors button cuts the selected clips in two at the playhead; M or the speaker button mutes them (press again to unmute).

Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo, for every change to tracks and clips.

Click or drag on the ruler to move through the video; hold it near an edge to scroll, faster the longer you hold. Times read min:sec:frame, using the video's frame rate.

The two export markers on the ruler set what Export saves: drag them, press I / O to place them at the playhead, double-click one to reset it.

Zoom with the slider, W / E, or Ctrl + mouse wheel over the timeline. Fully left shows the whole timeline; fully right shows 1 second.

The import buttons, above the track names and in the Clips list, add audio files at the playhead on the REC track, one after another. Drop audio anywhere in the app to do the same, or on a track to place it where you drop. Drop a video anywhere to choose it instead. Only the first supported file in a drop is imported.

The Clips list next to the video shows every clip in timeline order: click one to jump to its start and select it (Shift+click adds it to the selection), double-click to rename it. Each row has a WAV download button; the heading downloads all clips as a ZIP and can hide the list. The film button next to Choose video shows or hides the entire video and clips panel without changing playback or audio; this layout preference is remembered in the browser.

Mute silences a track in playback and leaves it out of the export.

The Video audio row at the top shows the sound of the video, so you can time your voice to it. It can't be edited; Export can include it.

The eye button above the track names opens Show or hide tracks: switch a track off to take it off the timeline and out of playback and both exports. Hiding is not deleting and not muting: the recordings, the volume and mute stay as they were, and a hidden track stays out of both exports until you show it again. Hiding the REC track moves REC to a visible track.
