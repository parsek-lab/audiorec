Saving & exporting

The project autosaves in this browser.

New project clears the video and all recordings — save a copy first if you want to keep them.

Save project writes a .audiorec file (video + recordings). The first time it asks where to save; after that — or after Open project — it overwrites the same file.

Export saves Audio only (a WAV of the unmuted tracks) or Video + audio (an MP4 with the original picture and the new mix). Choose the start and end; by default they are the two export markers on the timeline ruler, which start at 0 and at the timeline Length (the video length unless you set one). Silence is added where nothing plays. The video's own audio is mixed in only if you tick it.

Recording

Choose a video, enable REC on the tracks you want, then press Record and speak while it plays.

Space play / pause · * start / stop recording · W full screen

Mute on the Video audio row silences the video's own sound. It only affects what you hear; Export has its own option for the video audio.

    Editing tracks

    Drag a clip to move it; drag its edges to trim start and end. Drop it on another track to move it there at the same time position. Hold Alt while you start dragging to duplicate the selected clips instead.

    Double-click a track or clip name to rename it.

    Click a clip to select it, Shift+click to select more, or drag a box over empty space (Shift adds to the selection); Del or the trash button removes the selected clips, Esc clears the selection.

    C or the scissors button cuts the selected clips in two at the playhead; M or the speaker button mutes them (press again to unmute).

    Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo, for every change to tracks and clips.

    Click or drag on the ruler to move through the video; hold it near an edge to scroll, faster the longer you hold. Times read min:sec:frame, using the video's frame rate.

    The two export markers on the ruler set what Export saves: drag them, press I / O to place them at the playhead, double-click one to reset it.

    Zoom with the slider, + / -, or Ctrl + mouse wheel over the timeline. Fully left shows the whole video; fully right shows 1 second.

    The import button above the track names adds audio files at the playhead on the REC track, or drag files from your computer onto a track to place them where you drop.

    Mute silences a track in playback and leaves it out of the export.

    The Video audio row at the top shows the sound of the video, so you can time your voice to it. It can't be edited; Export can include it.
