How it works

From silent clip to finished narration in three steps.

1

Choose a video

Open any MP4, MOV or WebM file. Audiorec reads its frame rate, so the timeline counts in minutes, seconds and frames.

2

Record while it plays

Arm a track with REC and press *. You see your take being drawn live, and the level meter tells you if you're too quiet or clipping.

3

Edit and export

Trim, move, crossfade and glue clips, shape each track with volume, pan and effects, then export a WAV or an MP4 with the original picture, ready for your video editor.

Features

A small studio, without the setup.

Multi-track timeline

Separate tracks for each voice or for music, each with its own color, volume, pan, mute and effects.

Frame-accurate ruler

Times read mm:ss:ff. Zoom from the whole video down to one second.

Hear yourself live

Turn on Monitor to listen to the microphone in your headphones while you record.

Bring your own audio

Drag music or sound files onto a track and place them exactly where they belong.

Undo everything

Every move, trim, delete and rename can be undone and redone.

Projects that keep

Work autosaves in the browser. Save a .audiorec file to move a project to another computer.

Track effects

Equalizer with draggable bands, compressor, delay, reverb, pitch shifter, chorus and expander, in any order on every track.

Clean edits

Overlapping takes crossfade on their own. Set each clip's gain and fades, cut with C and glue the pieces back together with G.

Gap-free recording

The microphone is read directly, away from the busy page, so long takes keep every sample and stay in sync with the video.

Loop a section

Repeat playback between the export markers and rehearse a line until it lands.

Video or audio export

Export the mix as a WAV, or as an MP4 with the original picture, for exactly the range you mark.

Your look, your language

Dark, light and high-contrast accessible themes, and an interface in English, Italian, Spanish, Russian and Chinese.
